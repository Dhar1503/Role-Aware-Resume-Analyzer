M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering - admission 2026

Programme
A two-year taught programme covering advanced algorithms, systems and theory, with a one-year project.

Eligibility and selection
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, IT or a related engineering discipline
- Valid GATE score in Computer Science and Information Technology (CS)
- Shortlisting is based on GATE score; some specialisations hold a written test and interview

What we look for
- Strong foundation in algorithms, data structures, theory of computation and discrete mathematics
- Coursework in operating systems, computer architecture and compilers
- Programming ability in C or C++
- Prior projects or research exposure in systems, theory or machine learning is an advantage
