Recruitment of Graduate Engineers (Mechanical) through GATE 2026

Eligibility
- Full-time B.E./B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering with at least 65% marks (55% for SC/ST/PwBD)
- Valid GATE 2026 score in Mechanical Engineering (ME)
- Upper age limit 26 years (relaxation as per government norms)

Selection
Candidates are shortlisted on GATE 2026 score, followed by group discussion and personal interview.

Job profile
- Operation and maintenance of pumps, compressors and rotating equipment in refineries and pipelines
- Plant reliability, root-cause analysis and energy-efficiency improvements
- Design review using CAD tools and thermal/fluid analysis
- Industrial training or internship at a core-sector plant is desirable
